THE PROBLEM / THE METHOD

Project records are scattered.
Accountability should not be.

Commercial commitments rarely live in one place. The original scope may sit in a quotation, a change in an amendment, delivery in a handover note, and billing in a separate record.

CommitmentGraph creates a reviewable structure across those records. It shows what is recorded, what is connected, and where the evidence chain is incomplete. It does not decide who is liable.

BUILT AROUND EVIDENCE INTEGRITY

Keep the source.
Show the reasoning.

Uploaded originals are retained alongside extracted text. Each file receives a SHA-256 digest; original downloads are checked against that digest before delivery.

Findings are deterministic review signals. They do not establish fraud, prove financial loss, determine legal approval or replace professional judgement.

WHO IT IS FOR

For teams accountable
for project delivery.

Designed for project-based businesses, engineering firms and industrial suppliers that need to reconstruct commitments across fragmented records.

Typical reviewers: project controls, operations, commercial management and finance teams.

Deployment: self-hosted application with a SQLite database. Public multi-tenant SaaS, ERP integrations and role-based access are not part of v1.
