04 — EVIDENCE STANDARDS

Preserve the source.
Make the reasoning visible.

Original uploaded files are retained alongside extracted text. Each file receives a SHA-256 digest; downloads are checked against that digest.

Review signals are deterministic. They do not establish fraud, prove financial loss, decide legal approval or replace professional judgement.
